## Artificial Intelligence for Energy Systems

I am interested in applying machine learning and artificial intelligence to improve the reliability, efficiency, and sustainability of energy systems.

### Areas of interest

- Predictive maintenance
- Time-series and load forecasting
- Smart-grid analytics
- Power-system reliability
- Energy optimization
- Deep learning for energy applications
- Data-driven decision making
