I am an electrical engineer and data-science graduate based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I have served as an Assistant Engineer at Dhaka Power Distribution Company Limited (DPDC) since December 2019, following an earlier appointment as Assistant Engineer (Electrical) at Coal Power Generation Company Bangladesh Limited (CPGCBL).

I earned a Master’s in Applied Statistics and Data Science from Jahangirnagar University with a CGPA of 3.79/4.00 and a BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET).

My graduate project, **Predicting Vehicle Prices Using Machine Learning Techniques**, used 9,988 vehicle records and compared statistical and machine-learning approaches. The selected Random Forest model achieved an R² of 0.80.

This portfolio presents my verified education, professional experience, technical training, achievements, and project work.
